# Measurement and spend foundation

Slice A1 owns record construction/decoding, registered producer and policy identity,
discrete quantity-value admission, and pure resource/process/census validation. It creates no constitutional type and exposes no
admission, placement, throttle, retry, routing, standing, effect or mutation operation.

Burn evaluation, evidence-to-quantity binding, attribution, peer completeness, historical
reads and disposable read caches are explicitly deferred to `slice-A2`. The production
ingestion, pricing, qualified accounting, benchmark, process-observer, operator-surface and
spend-control seams named by Part 16 also remain dark until their owner contracts land.
`measurement.declarations.json` records that posture; an unavailable owner seam is never
represented by a package-local substitute.
