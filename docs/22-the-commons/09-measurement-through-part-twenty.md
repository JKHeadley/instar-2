## 9. Measurement through Part Twenty

**Rule — Part 20 owns counted populations and joins.** **Checks: P22-NF-09/16/17.**
The commons supplies registered event/evidence mappings, not a second `Measurement`, grade,
price, spend-control authority or route-support resolver. One local canonical opportunity
maps to the owners' accepted question/work identity; retry, child calls, multiple causes and
late corrections do not add opportunities. Production coverage counts accepted questions;
benchmark coverage counts the sealed `(run, scenario version, candidate, ordinal)` plan.
Refused, cancelled, pending, missing and conflicted planned members stay counted. Extra
unplanned executions are reported separately. A legitimate refusal can be correctly graded;
an unavailable outcome cannot be guessed into a pass.

Basis: Constraint 3; rules 13/39/41/58/69/75; Part 20 §7; Part 21 §9 at the section 1 pin; ME/J/G seams in section 14.

**Rule — comparison needs compatible owner evidence.** **Checks: P22-NF-09/16.**
Seven must resolve prompt/context/action-floor/schema/model/scenario/evaluation compatibility;
Nine supplies current complete conflict-free grades and benchmark evaluation; Ten supplies
current measured route support. An opaque digest or matching model name is not this tuple.
Private compatibility that cannot be shared stays locally attested and centrally non-comparable.
Observation windows include their start and exclude their end under comparable owner clocks;
late grades refine the original window. Bare timestamps or front arrival time cannot substitute.

Report proof, observation, attestation and inference separately, alongside interested-party
share, grader/model lineage, concentration, independence and missing peer reasons. Zero
population is undefined, missing price unknown, suppressed values not zero. Never average
local percentiles into a fleet percentile. A percentile is a value at or below which the
named fraction of observations falls. A complaint-enriched corpus is not the prevalence
of failure among all Instar users. Fleet support and strata (separately reported groups) preserve local ordinal standing;
equal operator weights in the pilot are a statistical setting, not authority equality.

Basis: Purpose evidence constraint; rules 13/58/75/86/108; Part 20 §7, P16-NF-31/32/38; R2/R4.

**Rule — measure the complete path and its costs.** **Checks: P22-NF-07/12/16/17/20.**
Publish separate counts and latency distributions for local capture, export, durable custody,
admission, owned review, real scenario promotion, proposal approval, release, local installation
and mature outcome. Include rejection, stale evidence, corrections, withdrawn support, silence
and missing follow-up. Count provider usage, actual price/settlement, storage, network, review
minutes and the cost of rejected/inconclusive work. E2's seven-day review disposition is not a
seven-day promise to fix every defect. A full store and zero owned work is a failed loop.

Basis: Coherency and wisdom, constraints 2/3; rules 39/41/58/75/85; R1 operating incident; R5 E2/E8/E12.
