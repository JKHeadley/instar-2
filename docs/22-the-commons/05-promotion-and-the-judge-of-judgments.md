## 5. Promotion and the judge of judgments

**Rule — promote a scenario and a proposal separately.** **Checks: P22-NF-09/10/11.**
A shared production benchmark scenario requires a real canonical case, a causally current
complete grade, Seven's scenario admission and compatibility, and permitted replay evidence.
A content-free complaint cannot be turned into an executable scenario by adding invented text.
Synthetic and sanitized-real artifacts keep distinct origin tags. A sanitized real replay
includes actual-input validation, decision-time frontier, author/audience bindings and a
semantic-loss record; future corrections remain grader-only. Prepared effects cannot dispatch.
Private evidence that remains local may support an attested result but no claim of public
reproducibility. Proposal B's isolation is required before remote invitations execute locally.

Basis: Big picture §8; rules 13/35/58/65/69/85/108; Part 21 §10 at the section 1 pin; R2/R5 benchmark gates.

**Rule — the constitutional-placement test judges the exact proposed behavior.** **Checks: P22-NF-10/11.**
Every lesson names its exact change and scope, purpose content hash, deciding purpose text,
pillar or constraint, the derivation from that text, all other floor checks, supporting and
disconfirming cases, conclusion/reason verdicts, human review owner and approval-bound artifact.
A tag saying “trust” without a derivation fails. A proposed learning must sit under a pillar;
a technical rule supplies its check and cannot replace that placement.

| Disposition | Deciding test | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| `placed` | Explicit derivation beneath a pillar, with no contrary floor | Eligible for evidence review; no automatic adoption |
| `contradiction` | Breaches a pillar, authority, scope or disclosure floor | Reject the learning; retain permitted evidence and dissent regardless of vote count |
| `gap` | Purpose does not decide the direction/value tradeoff | File candidate amendment with missing distinction, alternatives and cases; keep behavior inactive |
| `unproven` | Plausible placement but insufficient, refuted or conflicting evidence | Owned bounded investigation; no promotion claim |

An independent reproduced defect may open repair from one counterexample. The fleet lesson
claim still needs the pilot's independent-case support and all other gates. Urgency changes
review priority, never standing. Only the operator can change the judge, including when a
contradiction motivates a separately proposed constitutional amendment.

Basis: Purpose gap Rule, coherency/trust/sovereignty/alignment and “Who decides what”; big picture §8; R4 placement test; G1/G3.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 1: feedback is evidence, never authority.** **Checks: P22-NF-03/10/11.**
A report, score, benchmark win or unanimous cluster cannot create a grant, weaken a floor or
approve a release. Test repeated low-standing votes and a fluent proposed exception against
the same refusal as the single unsupported claim.

Basis: Purpose authority constraint and wisdom; rules 57/86; accepted seven-principle input recorded in R4/R5.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 2: every admitted grade carries verified standing.** **Checks: P22-NF-03/09.**
Resolve grader identity, relevant scope, delegation and currency independently of the payload.
An anonymous report remains useful evidence to investigate, but cannot become an accepted
grade by copying a verified grader's label. Standing and factual reliability remain separate.

Basis: Purpose wisdom and sovereignty; rules 28/29; section 3.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 3: local wisdom and fleet wisdom have different support.** **Checks: P22-NF-10/11/15.**
Local adaptation serves the operator within the constitution. Generalized fleet judgment
lessons require independent real cases, pillar placement and exact human approval. A local
style preference is not mandatory fleet policy. A pillar contradiction fails however many agree.

Basis: Sovereignty, trust/alignment and wisdom; big picture §§8/9; R5 E7; OD-04.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 4: later outcomes outrank immediate approval on the same factual question.** **Checks: P22-NF-08/09/17.**
Grade immediate, seven-day and 30-day evidence separately. Supported later evidence causally
updates the current grade; a newer unsupported opinion does not. Satisfaction cannot erase
harm or failed delivery. An unresolved outcome remains unknown, and subsequent harm reopens
support even after a trial ended.

Basis: Purpose wisdom, constraint 3; rules 24/58/108; section 10.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 5: conclusion and reason are separately falsifiable.** **Checks: P22-NF-08/09/10.**
Keep the conclusion, explanation, evidence and verdicts separate. Refuting the explanation
requires re-derivation even if the same conclusion may survive. A lucky action with a false
reason cannot satisfy the primary learning endpoint. Corrections invalidate downstream
support and preserve the earlier record.

Basis: Purpose wisdom and evidence constraint; rule 108; Nine's grade/correction authority.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 6: never optimize for approval.** **Checks: P22-NF-10/17.**
Use real outcome criteria, constitutional consistency, independent blinded review and frozen
hidden labels. Separate author from grader; calibrate graders on adjudicated cases. Test
opposite user beliefs, answer order and verbosity, justified disagreement and legitimate
withholding. Correlated model self-grades do not become independent support through repetition.

Basis: Purpose wisdom, trust/alignment and constraint 3; rules 24/65/108; R5 E4–E6.

**Rule — judgment-learning principle 7: the constitution judges judgments; only the operator changes it.** **Checks: P22-NF-10/11/12.**
Learning can propose a changed rule with evidence and exact versioned text. It cannot write
governing material, change the rubric of its own success, or treat a reward as permission.
Changed criteria require a new sealed evaluation and fresh holdout, not a new verdict on the
same inspected outcomes. Every unresolved policy question is recorded in section 15.

Basis: Purpose gap Rule and operator-only authority; big picture §8; rules 90/94/108; G1–G4.
