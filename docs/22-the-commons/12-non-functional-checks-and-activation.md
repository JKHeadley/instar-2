**Rule — activation has separate complete arms.** **Checks: P22-NF-09/13/14/17/20.**
A requires NF-02–12 and NF-15–20, with NF-09 limited to admitted local replay and shared
scenario validation; it does not execute invitations from B. NF-09's EV key includes the
isolation/decision-time local replay half for A. B adds its invitation/repeat-budget half,
NF-13 and E9, retaining every A floor. C stays a separate inactive option until NF-14 and
E10 pass with G2 approval; any proposed change arising from C still passes A. NF-17 has
separate A, B and C reports; NF-20 proves composition independently for each activated arm.
The operator's build/activation, privacy, risk and spend conditions are additional to owner
landing. A declined or unavailable optional arm is reported unexecuted, never passed.

Basis: Purpose authority/evidence constraints; R5 staged recommendation; G1–G4; section 8.

**Rule — test the environment that the claim names.** **Checks: P22-NF-07/13/14/19/20.**
Record package/owner generations, machine/incarnation, configured storage/loss model, load,
rate settings, network faults and resource ceilings. Exercise real authenticated interfaces,
production startup, owner delegation and crash/restart with finite deadlines. Tier 1 model
supervision records validation of each critical step through Seven; a no-op dependency or a
configured supervisor name cannot satisfy the consequential pipeline. Models may narrow actions
but fixed identity, privacy, money and approval checks remain authoritative. Stress attempt
counts complement semantic cases; rejecting all positives does not pass. Fresh running proofs
and an independently reachable status path are mandatory for ongoing service.

Basis: Purpose's sole consequential-effect definition; rules 34/38/43/47/62/65/69/77/95/111; E12.
