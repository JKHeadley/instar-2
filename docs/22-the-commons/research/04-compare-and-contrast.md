Instar trains model weights, has an RL reward model, or has measured fleet wisdom.

## What the two tiers need together

**Rule — the local loop remains complete when the commons is absent.** **Check:** disable
enrollment and disconnect the front; capture, diagnosis, grade correction, local proposal,
verification and recovery still work, with no export and no blocked principal response.
The local source records stay with their owners. The front stores permitted derivatives, not a
replacement memory. Grounds: `2x:docs/04-the-big-picture.md:323–334` and
`docs/00-the-purpose.md:111–127`; derived schema and privacy checks are in
[R2](02-what-flows-and-what-must-not.md).

**Rule — ownership travels with a case.** **Check:** each proposed adapter names its real
owner port and unavailable state; absent seams cannot be replaced by commons-owned copies.
Part 21 supplies recall stages and local replay validation; Part 23 supplies sensitivity and
use/withhold assessment; Seven supplies judgment input and scenario identity; Nine supplies
grades and benchmark evaluation; Twenty supplies counted populations and cost joins; the
effect doorway owns export/release admission. Part 22 proposes front custody, admission,
clustering and distribution. These are design boundary assignments awaiting owner acceptance,
not claims that all producers are executable. Part 20 expressly identifies missing benchmark
resolution and route-support seams
(`2x:docs/20-measurement-ledgers/07-feature-benchmark-and-burn-joins.md:42–78`).

**Rule — content-free signals and replayable evidence stay separate.** **Check:** a derived
cause label never becomes a shared executable scenario merely because a cluster is large.
An admitted real scenario requires its actual-input witness, outcome grade, owner validation,
export grant and transformation-loss record; synthetic fixtures retain a separate label.
Private originals may support a locally attested result without becoming inspectable at the
front. Unknown comparability or independence stays unknown. Grounds:
`P21:10-the-pilot-human-comparison-and-incident-replay.md:117–205`,
`2x:docs/04-the-big-picture.md:299–311`, and
`docs/20-measurement-ledgers/07-feature-benchmark-and-burn-joins.md:16–40,99–109`.

**Rule — collective custody has a loss detector and an accountable next step.** **Check:**
persist the local export before transmission; use destination-bound idempotency, durable
front receipts, fenced processing and append-only assessment succession. Detect expired
outboxes, missing receipt reconciliation, stalled review and broken proposal ownership.
Clustering records its algorithm/version, member identities, splits, disagreement and curated
decisions; it never turns similarity into truth. A correction invalidates dependent grades,
scenarios and proposed releases until re-evaluated. This extends purpose constraint 2 and
the factory's idempotent owned-work boundary, rather than equating a full inbox with learning.

**Rule — one address selects a commons; it does not transfer trust.** **Check:** the identical
receiver/processor/package runs against two isolated stores, one developer-operated and one
operator-operated. A destination change stops the old queue, requires destination-scoped
enrollment and consent, and does not forward old richer payloads or inherit signing trust.
Provisioning storage, keys and an active review owner precedes the address flip. Cross-commons
forwarding is a new effect. Threadline is a transport candidate under
`2x:docs/04-the-big-picture.md:344–350`, not an admission authority or a prerequisite for
using the local loop. Protocol parity and authorization decide transport eligibility.

## Standing and weighting without majority capture

**Rule — verify standing before admitting a grade as a grade.** **Check:** an envelope's
signature binds bytes to a destination-scoped enrollment; a separate verified grant binds
grader, principal role, subject scope, delegation, validity interval and revocation state.
Caller-supplied `operator` is insufficient. The receiving front keeps a bounded unverified
signal lane for anonymous reports; those reports contribute no accepted grade or independent
operator count until verified. They can still trigger an independently reproduced defect.
Grounds: `2x:docs/01-the-rules.md:268–269` and purpose `:62–64,125–127`.

**Rule — apply operator > verified user > other agent > anonymous fleet within the relevant
scope.** **Check:** use the following ordered standing classes in admission and review,
and test both a legitimate scope match and a falsely claimed one for each row.

| Class | Weight in a scoped judgment | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | First consideration for preferences, criteria and authorized local adaptation within the pillars | Being one agent's operator does not confer another operator's scope or ownership of another person's confidential material |
| Verified user | Next consideration for that user's experience and decisions covered by their grants | A user identity does not create operator authority or authorize disclosure to a different audience |
| Other agent | Advisory diagnosis or grade under its verified delegation; retain model lineage and interested-party status | A delegated operator action uses the recorded grant; an agent cannot promote its own role by assertion |
| Anonymous fleet | Discovery signal only; lowest review standing, no authority-bearing grade | Thousands of submissions do not manufacture verified standing or independent operators |

**Rule — standing weight is ordinal precedence, not an exchange rate for votes.** **Check:**
the admission decision uses this sequence: constitutional floor and scope; verified standing;
evidence eligibility/currentness; independent outcome evaluation; human promotion authority.
Lower-standing volume cannot offset a higher-standing scoped decision. A queue may order
ordinary eligible cases by standing, but any credible floor-violation signal has a separate
bounded investigation lane so a rare anonymous counterexample is not starved. Equal-standing
conflict remains disputed pending scoped adjudication. There is no global sum such as
ten agent votes equaling one operator vote, and no cross-operator winner-takes-all ballot.
This implements the seven-principle input under purpose `:39–74,140–151`; it does not
silently choose numerical moral weights.

**Rule — authority over a choice is distinct from reliability about facts.** **Check:**
preserve every assessment with separate conclusion grade, reason grade, evidence strength,
outcome horizon, grader reliability, conflict and currentness. A reproducible agent-supplied
counterexample can refute an operator's factual explanation without granting that agent policy
authority. An operator's preferences can govern a local choice without changing an observed
failure into success. A later outcome-supported assessment outranks an immediate approval on
the same factual question; a newer unsupported opinion does not. Arrival order never resolves
conflicting current grades. A refuted reason forces re-derivation even if the conclusion survives.
Grounds: `2x:docs/00-the-purpose.md:53–65`, `docs/01-the-rules.md:237,340` and
`docs/20-measurement-ledgers/07-feature-benchmark-and-burn-joins.md:23–27,99–109`.

**Value — weight fleet estimates by independent operator clusters within evidence strata.**
For the proposed evaluation, compute each verified operator's paired outcome delta first,
then give independent operators equal weight in the same comparable stratum. Report standing
classes and proof/observation/attestation/inference separately. Duplicate agents, retries,
shared incident origins and common grader/model ancestry do not add independent votes.
Unknown operator independence excludes a case from an independence-dependent promotion claim,
while preserving it as a signal. Equal cluster weights are an experimental estimator, not
equal authority over local choices. R5 fixes concentration and sample thresholds before use.

**Rule — grade the grader without letting the grader choose the judge.** **Check:** use a
frozen outcome rubric, blinded candidate identities, separate proposer and evaluator, hidden
real-case labels, answer-order reversal and independently adjudicated calibration cases.
Track conclusion and reason error separately by standing/evidence class and outcome horizon.
Record incentives and common-model lineage; correlated AI grades are not independent human
review. An unreliable grader loses evidential eligibility, not the principal's constitutional
rights. Changing the rubric reopens affected comparisons, never retroactively improves a score.
Grounds: rule 65 at `2x:docs/01-the-rules.md:221`, rule 108 at `:340`, and the operator's
seven principles. R3's judge-bias and sycophancy findings motivate these proposed controls;
they do not establish an Instar grader's measured accuracy.

## Constitutional placement as an admission record

**Rule — place the exact behavioral lesson, not just its topic.** **Check:** every promotion
record carries the proposed change and scope; evidence and disconfirming cases; purpose hash;
deciding pillar/constraint with an explicit derivation; checks against all other pillars and
permission floors; separate conclusion/reason verdicts; human owner; and approval bound to the
exact artifact. A tag saying `trust` without an explanation fails. The test has four dispositions:

| Disposition | Deciding test | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `placed` | A purpose statement, pillar or constraint decides the change and no other floor is contradicted | Eligible for evidence review, never automatically adopted |
| `contradiction` | The lesson would breach an existing pillar, scope or permission floor | Reject the learning as proposed; retain permitted evidence and dissent; popularity cannot reopen admission |
| `gap` | The existing purpose does not decide the direction/value tradeoff | File a candidate purpose amendment with the missing distinction, alternatives and affected cases; keep the proposed change inactive |
| `unproven` | Placement is plausible but the case, cause, grade or independence is insufficient or conflicted | Preserve a bounded investigation with an owner; do not promote a policy or claim wisdom |

This is the check required by `2x:docs/00-the-purpose.md:67–74,147–151`. A separate human
may propose a constitutional amendment even after a contradiction, but a commons learning
cannot amend its judge. Only the operator can change the purpose (`:140–145`). An unresolved
question must be decided or recorded as a candidate amendment for later design convergence;
recording a gap does not authorize implementing the proposed answer.

**Rule — exercise placement on adversarial as well as attractive lessons.** **Check:** the
following fixture expectations hold even after duplicating supporting submissions 10,000 times.

| Proposed learning | Placement and disposition |
|---|---|
| Preserve failed recall cases before a retry can overwrite them | Coherency and constraint 2; `placed`, contingent on evidence and owner implementation |
| Use a permitted private fact internally while withholding it from an unauthorized audience | Trust/alignment and wisdom; `placed` only with distinct private-use permission and reason/outcome evidence |
| Disclose a protected fact because the recipient rated the answer highly | Trust/alignment and non-widening authority; `contradiction`, however high the grader's standing |
| Mark a commitment complete when approval rose but delivery failed later | Constraint 3 and wisdom; `contradiction`; later outcome invalidates apparent immediate success |
| Adopt a local operator's concise style across every agent | Sovereignty; local adaptation can be `placed`, mandatory fleet preference is outside scope |
| Set a universal numerical exchange rate between confidentiality cost and withholding harm when both choices meet floors | `gap`: wisdom names both costs but supplies no universal exchange rate; file an operator question instead of inventing one |

## Consequential effects and the return boundary

**Rule — classify commons effects by all four purpose tests.** **Check:** at effect-kind
registration and each scoped invocation record whether the agent alone can undo it, whether it
exceeds an operator-named resource threshold, whether it exceeds granted scope, and whether it
touches operator-marked policy sensitivity (`2x:docs/00-the-purpose.md:76–92`). Export to a
front generally cannot be undone by the agent alone because the receiver has custody; label it
consequential even if content-free. Publication, release and authority changes retain their own
tests. A sandboxed local replay can be ordinary if none of the four tests holds; a large paid
evaluation can be consequential even without disclosure. Being agent-to-agent traffic can make
an effect non-user-facing while it remains consequential. No new commons-specific meaning of
significant or critical is introduced.

**Rule — a fleet release is a proposal at the receiving agent until locally admitted.**
**Check:** verify signer, artifact version/hash, placement, evidence, human approval and local
compatibility/scope before installation. A valid front signature authenticates origin, not
policy permission. A release cannot rewrite pillars, widen a grant, turn sharing on, or disable
local loss detection. Withdrawn evidence or a harmful later outcome marks dependent packages
for re-evaluation with a visible hold/rollback decision and receipts; originals and historical
verdicts remain attributable. Missing export or release authorization fails closed; an absent
commons does not close the local user-response path (rule 95 at `docs/01-the-rules.md:303`).

**Value — carry these comparisons into a bounded choice.** R5 compares a case-and-proposal
commons, a federated evaluation extension and an aggregate privacy option under the same
standing, outcome and placement gates. Useful fleet size, effect on delayed judgment outcomes,
private-case comparability and acceptable privacy loss remain unknown. A measured improvement
inside one population is evidence to review, never a mandate from that population.
