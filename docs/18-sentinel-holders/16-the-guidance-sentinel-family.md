## 16. The guidance sentinel family

**Value — guidance makes the agent more useful.** Shared review notices needless deferral,
unsupported claims, ignored corrections and disclosures that exceed the recipient's standing.
It informs the agent's next judgment and preserves lessons across sessions. It is not a second
sender, a watcher per rule or a permission system for ordinary conversation.

**Rule — judge patterns in retrospect; judge moments live only when irreversible.** Rules
4, 10, 12, 41, 57, 58, 66, 77, 86, 95 and 116; **checks: P14-NF-70/71/76–78 and P7-NF-06/20**.
Nine's existing retrospective review reads full conversation context, delivered reply evidence,
standing and source permissions through the record read port. Seven admits and meters these
system-initiated calls. A model-written reply does not require a Jev verdict, a second full-context
review or a rewrite-review pass before sending. Optional advisory pre-send review uses Seven's
registered deliver-with-flags/no-objection default. Unavailable, uncertain or undecided advice
is recorded as such and does not replace the answer with a holding note.

Live enforcement remains at its existing owners: secrets, spend and stop; verified standing and
least revelation; exact effect identity and durable cause; and supervision of a specifically
registered irreversible moment. The fact that a platform send is irreversible does not turn
all wording into a mandatory semantic review. The purpose fixes sign-off, not a general review
veto. A source-specific disclosure needing judgment is resolved with permitted context before
that disclosure; unresolved permission withholds the affected private detail, not unrelated help.
Audience and source standing come from current authenticated records, not a model's guess.
A private operator chat does not imply entitlement to every third-party source. Neither a model
pass nor missing review grants disclosure permission. No automatic content-free holding note
or sentence-deletion engine substitutes for the agent's scoped judgment.

| Member | Evidence reviewed together | Result owned by the existing feedback/work path |
|---|---|---|
| Tone and self-stop | Actual request, role/tools, reasoning claims and run disposition | An avoidable refusal becomes owned work; a real limit keeps its evidence and investigation. |
| Deferral | Promise text, durable commitments, due work and observed result | Missing capture or follow-through becomes repair; a reviewer never marks work fulfilled. |
| Claim verification | Self-state/capability records and what the recipient actually received | Unsupported certainty is corrected; uncertainty stays explicit. |
| Correction learning | Verified correction/preference, later context and later behavior | Source-linked lesson is delivered and its later use checked, not merely counted as remembered. |
| Sensitivity | Verified audience, source permissions, directives and disclosed content | Repair and future scoped guidance; retrospective review cannot undo a disclosure. |

**Rule — record the finding, response and outcome separately.** Rules 9, 26, 39, 42, 58,
85, 93 and 108; **checks: P14-NF-72/74/75**. The family uses Nine's review records and existing
history, never a guidance store. Each member records asked/clear/finding/undecided/unavailable,
its source ids and separate conclusion/reason, and the agent's acceptance or reasoned disagreement.
Not asked is never clear. A proposed correction, context delivery and an actually delivered
correction are different facts. Only the transport evidence for the same immutable part supports
a delivered claim; a pre-dispatch send record or worker exit cannot. Preference recurrence uses
source-linked meaning and current standing; it cannot create authority from agent-authored text.
A confidential instruction persists under Rule 93 until superseded or completed.

**Rule — prove both advice availability and actual floor enforcement.** Rules 34, 36, 43,
62 and 107; **checks: P14-NF-70–78**. Implementation replays real recorded summary outputs,
Jev uncertain/undecided verdicts, full-context review shapes and delivered replies including empty
bubbles, recording source update ids. Advisory outage must still deliver an already-permitted
answer, while a secret or standing-uncovered disclosure must refuse the affected content and
preserve its cause. A safe group answer proceeds without a mandatory reviewer; an unauthorized
private detail does not. A sent correction needs its receipt; an unreceipted candidate stays
unconfirmed. Review/repair loops have finite caps, backoff and an owned remainder. The shared
review checks later actual behavior, not only the originating reply or a synthetic success.

**Value — delivery and limits.** `step6-grounding` owns the retrospective guidance consumer,
using `journal-read-port-1013` and current summaries until the verified step-5 cutover; `step2-doors`
owns send evidence. Existing review captures seed acceptance, but historical preview counts do
not establish performance of this continuing path. Retrospective judgment may miss a pattern;
coverage and unavailable evidence stay visible. No model-facing implementation, recorded replay
or live guidance result is claimed by this design.
