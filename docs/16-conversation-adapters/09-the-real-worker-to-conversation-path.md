## 9. The real worker-to-conversation path

**Rule — every harness uses the common continuing-turn contract.** Rules 29, 30, 63, 75,
105 and 115; **checks: P12-NF-42/43 and P13-NF-18–24/40/47**. Ten's public
`describe/launch/deliver/observe/control` port is the only worker contract. Claude Code, Codex
and native translate it inside their adapters. Four and the conversation owner retain accepted
input and deduplication; Five retains bounded work; Ten/Thirteen retain admitted worker and
observation identities. The worker has scoped tools and account handles, not platform credentials,
raw account sessions or a private sender. Hooks alone are not physical mediation evidence.

A turn may reason, use tools and return reply content through multiple inner calls. Seven owns
system-initiated questions; inner paid calls are admitted before dispatch and reported individually
through the adapter as Seven section 3 specifies. A turn-end event is not a delivered reply, and
an empty result is not a completed reply obligation. A new process gets a fresh admitted incarnation;
restart preserves input, turn, call and outward-operation identities and all outstanding exposure.

**Rule — a turn preserves causal order without fixing the number of calls or replies.** Rules
14, 15, 26, 28, 33, 41, 42, 46, 58, 62, 63, 68, 75 and 89;
**checks: P12-NF-28/43/44 and P13-NF-18–27**.

1. Capture the authenticated event before platform acknowledgment. Four deduplicates its stable
   event identity, resolves current binding/standing and appends intake. A repeated phrase in a
   new event is new input; it is not transport duplication.
2. The conversation owner records the turn and queues busy inputs durably. Five opens or attaches
   bounded work only when needed. The coordinator owns the bounded conversational acknowledgment
   and fast-answer suppression under section 7; a platform receipt is not that acknowledgment.
3. At actual start, resume or compaction, Five/awareness resolve complete history or valid covered
   summaries, current clock, open work, standing and capabilities. Ten delivers the current context
   specification; the adapter witnesses consumption. The launch's old input is not current grounding.
4. Deliver the distinct input through the common adapter. Busy-not-dispatched stays queued;
   uncertain consumption is reconciled under the same delivery id, never blindly redelivered.
   The worker continues within the remaining bounds. Model calls use per-call admission/accounting;
   scoped reversible tools retain their whole cause and enforced bounds, while irreversible actions
   require exact per-operation durable preparation before dispatch. Neither a tool hook nor a
   transcript can confer account authority.
5. Consume source-bound reply-candidate, progress, terminal, usage and control observations using
   the durable owner cursor. The session may continue reasoning after a candidate; a terminal event
   supplies neither send evidence nor task completion. Seven's system-question acceptance path is
   one producer of usable output, not a mandatory extra model call/review for every worker answer.
6. The conversation owner prepares permitted, attributable content under section 7's reply obligation,
   immutable part and attempt identities. Eight/Six validate exact rendered bytes, authority, scope,
   binding, fence, stop, generation, expiry, bounds and full causal durability before committing and
   consuming the dispatch claim. Recheck current admission after durability waits and before the
   confined conversation custodian invokes the concrete adapter once. Advisory guidance follows
   Seven's deliver-with-flags/no-objection default; secrets and exact disclosure/authority floors hold.
7. Record provider response or uncertainty. Nine assesses the demanded delivery stage, Eight settles
   and Six accounts the same operation. Five accepts actual work results separately. Duplicate
   observations join the existing facts; unknown delivery cannot be retried under a new part or turn.
   Proven non-occurrence supports only section 7's governed corrected-rendering successor.
8. Retain the conversation and unfinished obligations for the next input. Stop closes new affected
   dispatch without deleting intake; a finite session limit queues an admitted continuation rather
   than claiming completion. Rebuild every view from retained causal facts after a cut.
