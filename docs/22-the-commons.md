# Part twenty-two — the commons

**Value — purpose.** Every Instar agent should be able to turn real failures, sound decisions,
benchmark results and corrections into useful learning for itself and, with permission, for
other agents. A commons preserves accountable evidence and returns tested proposals. Coherence
and wisdom improve only when later outcomes justify that claim. Popularity cannot grant
permission or change the constitution.

Basis: [Purpose](00-the-purpose.md), coherency, wisdom and sovereignty; [big picture](04-the-big-picture.md) §§8–9; accepted [research recommendation](22-the-commons/research/05-proposals-and-evaluation.md).

**Rule — reading convention and evidence discipline.** **Checks: P22-NF-01–20.** Every claim
belongs to its nearest Rule or Value block. Rules name proposed checks; Values name choices
or aspirations. This design selects Proposal A, the accountable case-and-proposal commons.
Proposal B, locally admitted federated evaluation, stays gated on owner seams, isolation and
incremental benefit. Proposal C, privacy-bounded aggregate prevalence, stays separate and
deferred and can never promote a judgment lesson by vote. Named checks are not evidence of
implementation, activation, independent convergence or operator approval. Supporting research
supplies evidence; the indexed sections supply the complete proposed contract.

Basis: Purpose constraints 1–4 and authority boundary; rules 13/49/69/90/91/95/108/111; section 12 activation and section 15 decisions.
