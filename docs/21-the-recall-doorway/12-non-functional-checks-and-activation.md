A report records each named arm separately. A/B activation can pass with the live arm recorded
inactive because approval is absent or declined; it cannot claim that the live positive ran.
Live activation requires both complete arms. No report may silently shorten a whole positive,
substitute a schema check for runtime evidence, or label the combined check passed when only
one arm executed. Every arm remains non-executable until its complete section-14 dependency
list lands, including its cited scopes in `seam-response-recall-doorway-grants.md` and inherited
grant files. The live arm additionally remains inert until its current approvals are present.

**Rule — activation follows actual owner composition.** Rules 34, 44, 47, 62, 65, 69,

Implementation adds agent awareness, capability inventory, operator context/coherence views,
mobile-complete pending decisions and migration parity through the owning templates/update path.
This documentation-only draft does not edit those owners or claim those consumers exist.
Feature posture is **design-only, inactive**. Proposed maturation is test installation, then
development installation, then fleet only on each applicable check and real surface proof.
At implementation admission, the owner must assign a named graduation/recheck date and accountable
reviewer; an unassigned date inhibits activation rather than becoming an indefinite dark feature.
The A/B + retrospective profile requires all applicable whole checks and all five A/B arms
above, including A4a's real consumers; it has no A4b/V dependency. The live-review profile
additionally requires all five live arms, A4b/V and their current approvals. The production
assembly registers the exact profile and required checks; an inactive optional live arm does
not inhibit A/B, and an absent required A/B consumer does. Section 14 is the dependency authority.

**Value — honest partial delivery is inspectable.** Existing owner fixtures can prove that a
dependency shape is understood. They cannot prove that a person received a coherent answer,
that compaction accounting landed, or that another machine's private history is safe to use.
Review reports mark those arms unavailable until the precise seam and real composition pass.
