## 1. Authority, evidence and the question being compared

**Value — scope.** Part Twenty-Three asks how an agent should use what it is
permitted to know: assess sensitivity, consider both sharing and withholding,
choose an action, record a testable reason, and learn from outcomes under the
standing of the people who assess them. Complete relevant recall is the baseline,
including non-obvious connections; accurate retrieval alone cannot answer this
question. This is the purpose's wisdom Value, not a claim of achieved wisdom.

## 3. The permission floor and the exact Part 21 seam

**Rule — wisdom narrows permitted choices and cannot widen permission.**
Rules 28/29/57/86/95 and purpose authority constraint; **proposed check:
P23-C-02**, compose each proposal with the Part 21 §5 positive and negative
boundary cases. Part 21 owns authorized recall and source-owner resolution
before candidate access, graph hops, originals, summaries, embeddings, rerankers,
provider input, cache reuse and final recipient binding. Unauthorized source
existence, counts and topology remain protected. Part 23 must not first read
forbidden content in order to assess how sensitive it is.

Part 21 §15 OD-02 says: private use needs its own permission, disclosure needs
separate permission, and unestablished permission means private and unused.
Its follow-on recommendation explicitly assigns the sensitivity, harm balancing,
recording and outcome learning layer to “judgment of use.” Part 23 consumes only
the permitted evidence and current scope decisions. The source custodian may
attach a restricted assessment in its own authorized context; downstream agents
receive only permitted fields or an opaque status, never an existence leak.

**Value — proposed exchange.** The recall owner supplies authorized fact/version
handles, the actual-input manifest reference, source/identity provenance, current
scope decisions, freshness and permitted uncertainty diagnostics. Part 23 adds
sensitivity-assessment references and a use-or-withhold Decision linked to that
manifest. The effect owner consumes the Decision within its own current floor,
rechecking destination and authority. This is a requested composition through
public owner ports, not an implemented additional Part 21 endpoint.

An assessment records who a fact concerns, the source's standing and reliability
as distinct dimensions, possible harms to each party from sharing and withholding,
and observable inferences from the proposed action. A fact about two people does
not become solely the operator's because the operator reported it. Derived facts
retain contributing provenance and the intersection of applicable permissions.
Unknown sensitivity never becomes a public label through missing data.

**Rule — separately permitted internal use requires enforceable composition.**
**Proposed check: P23-C-03**, a private-use-only fact cannot enter a provider or
principal context until that destination is authorized and the composed consumer
can constrain downstream use. Consider a fictional private scheduling concern:
choosing an otherwise ordinary meeting time may help without explanation, but
the selection itself can reveal the concern to someone who knows the alternatives.
The relevant audience includes people observing timing, tool arguments, invited
participants and later explanations. No redacted name or model assurance proves
non-disclosure. Unenforceable modes stay inactive under Part 21, while separately
authorized general assistance remains available.

## 4. Three complete approaches to compare

**Value — proposals, not deployed controls.** Each approach must meet the same
permission, recording, standing and constitutional requirements. The differences
are the amount and timing of contextual deliberation. R5 specifies the records
and an evaluation that can reject all three.

| Required dimension | A — fact cards and retrospective review | B — contextual use assessment | C — independent challenge of permitted options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-fact sensitivity | Versioned card on every learned fact: affected subjects, source standing, typical audiences and sharing/withholding harms; context delta at each use | Same card plus explicit assessment of each proposed flow, combination of facts and observable action | B's assessment plus a separately authorized challenger examining missed subjects, inferences and harms |
| Recorded judgment | Compact Decision at every use/withhold occasion: actual inputs, chosen mode, separate reason, floor result and outcome hook | Full alternatives: disclose minimum necessary, use privately, withhold, seek permitted clarification or provide general help; harms and defeating evidence recorded | B plus the challenger's independent objection and disposition; disagreement preserved rather than voted away |
| Standing and grades | Existing Grade, extended with scope-verified standing; local preference learning after review; eligible candidate offered to Part 22 | Same owner Grade, with per-party harm claims, delayed outcomes and bounded local lesson context | Same, but challenger quality also graded; neither two models nor repeated votes create extra standing |
| Grade the grader / resist approval | Frozen labels, independent sampled audit, causal grade corrections; approval is only a tagged observation | A plus pre-outcome reason review, contrary-evidence tests and paired pressure probes | Independent initial verdicts before debate, model/author blinding and correlation reporting; audit the adjudicator too, on a bounded sample |
| Pillar placement | Each local lesson names its deciding pillar and scope, or becomes an explicit gap; no automatic fleet promotion | Explicit entailment, counterexample and conflict review; rejected contradiction remains evidence | Competing pillar arguments retained; authority stays with the constitution/operator, never a jury majority |
| Likely advantage, unmeasured | Lowest extra deliberation cost and smallest context duplication | Makes both harms and indirect disclosure inspectable at the decision that matters | May reveal a missed perspective or weak justification in contested cases |
| Likely failure, unmeasured | A stable card can miss a novel audience inference or dangerous combination | Fluent structured reasons can rationalize a poor choice; incomplete context still matters | Added exposure, latency and correlated mistakes; a debate can reward persuasion |
| Live posture | Record within existing decision/effect paths; retrospective semantic review | Same; no mandatory extra semantic blocking review for ordinary chat | Offline/background initially; live challenge requires an exact already-approved exception and measured benefit |

**Value — A is a serious control, not permission-free labeling.** A static
public/private bit without subjects, withholding harm, source standing or use
context would not meet the brief and is not among the candidates. A tests whether
an inexpensive fact card plus existing contextual reasoning and retrospective
grading is sufficient. B tests the additional value of explicit alternatives and
per-flow reasoning. C tests whether independent challenge earns its additional
cost after B has already made those alternatives visible.

**Value — no fourth privacy shortcut.** Replacing exact memories with noisy
facts or aggregating positive feedback is not an equivalent proposal. It changes
the recall contract or substitutes a proxy for wisdom. A bounded private-release
mechanism may become part of the commons' export design; it cannot replace this
agent's use decision or source permission.

## 5. Grading is not approval, and neither is authority

**Rule — assess claims within standing, preserving unsupported reports.**
Rules 28/29/58/85/108; **proposed check: P23-C-04**, inject forged operator
claims, a real operator outside the relevant subject scope, expert evidence with
no permission power, and anonymous reproducible defect evidence. A report can
start an investigation without qualifying as an authoritative grade. Verify the
principal and their standing for the particular criterion, subject, relationship
and time. Record evidence strength and competence separately; standing does not
make an empirical assertion true. Feedback outside standing remains evidence
with an honest status, never discarded or quietly promoted.

An affected person can supply evidence of harm they experienced without gaining
control of another person's consent. The operator can express their own desired
interaction without authorizing third-party disclosure. An independent expert
can assess a factual premise without changing the purpose. An automated grader
needs delegated grading scope and permitted evidence, and its semantic verdict
remains inference. The agent that chose the action cannot certify its own
independence by spawning another instance of the same evaluation process.

**Rule — later outcomes outrank immediate reactions as evidence about those
outcomes, not by timestamp alone.** **Proposed check: P23-C-05**, a happy
immediate reply followed by supported harm must reopen the case and invalidate
dependent learning support; a late unsupported compliment cannot erase observed
harm. Nine owns causal reassessment and preserves old grades. Reason refutation
forces re-derivation even when the conclusion still looks right. Missing evidence
means unverifiable, not contradicted; a counterfactual benefit is not observed
merely because the selected action succeeded. Distinct affected parties and
conflicting current grades remain separate rather than averaged into a pass.

## 6. Division of labour with the commons

**Rule — Part 23 owns per-agent judgment and its grading semantics; Part 22 owns
fleet admission.** **Proposed check: P23-C-06**, trace a local learning through
submission, refusal, correction and fleet return without letting Part 23 issue a
fleet admission or an export grant. The architecture below is a proposed seam
against the pinned commons research, not an assignment already granted by it.

| Responsibility | Owner and boundary |
|---|---|
| Capture, identity, permission and recall | Source/intake owners and Part 21; preserve exact permitted originals and current scope |
| Per-agent sensitivity, use judgment, wisdom criteria and local learning content | Part 23, through Seven's Decision/manifest and Nine's Grade/feedback/re-derivation machinery; no second journal or grade authority |
| Outcome witnessing and open improvement work | Effect/verification/run owners; a lesson cannot close a failed effect or a correction-class review |
| Canonical populations, compatibility and coverage | Part 20; Part 23 supplies criteria and consumes measurements, including missingness |
| Candidate offered for sharing | Part 23 prepares a scoped learning proposal, separate conclusion/reason grades, standing evidence, pillar placement or gap, negative cases, limitations and correction lineage; source/effect owners still decide export eligibility |
| Wire schema, destination enrollment, privacy transformations and receipts | Part 22's commons contract; content-free fields by default after its applicable enrollment policy, richer material only under separate permission |
| Cross-agent independence, cohort sufficiency, admission weighting and fleet human approval | Part 22; it evaluates many independent cases, pillar compatibility and human approval before fleet admission. Part 23 sets no competing fleet quorum, reputation score, privacy budget or admission threshold |
| Consuming an admitted lesson locally | Part 23 rechecks current local scope, pillar version, applicability and evidence status; fleet acceptance supplies evidence, not broader local authority |
| Withdrawal or later refutation | Part 23 invalidates dependent local support and emits an eligible correction; Part 22 propagates fleet disposition through its owned correction/withdrawal contract |

**Value — why the separation matters.** One operator's repeated feedback can
improve that operator's experience within the constitution. It cannot become
many independent fleet cases by being copied to several agents or machines.
Part 23 retains the local independence and limitation evidence; Part 22 decides
whether its admissible cross-agent evidence supports a fleet claim. If privacy
prevents proof of independence, mark it unknown. Never export raw private
provenance just to improve an admission score. Local learning works while fleet
sharing is disabled or unavailable.

The commons' closed export contract excludes raw lesson prose, reasons and
private hashes by default. A Part 23 candidate is therefore a local object first;
it is not serialized wholesale. Part 22 may receive only permitted categories
and opaque destination-scoped identifiers. A richer lesson or replay needs its
separate export path and semantic-loss declaration. No data export occurs in
this research round.

## 7. Pillar placement and the undecidable remainder

**Rule — a pillar citation requires an argument and a counterexample.** Purpose
gap rule and Rules 49/108; **proposed check: P23-C-07**, review each learning's
parent statement, scoped claim, supporting cases, falsifying evidence and conflict
search. A matching word such as “trust” does not prove placement.

| Learning candidate, fictional examples | Deciding basis and required limit |
|---|---|
| Remember that this operator prefers a short answer unless comparing alternatives | Coherency and wisdom: durable, useful personal context; local preference only, no concealment of necessary evidence |
| Use a permitted private constraint to avoid an insensitive suggestion without revealing its cause | Trust and authority constraint: both the private-use permission and enforceable non-disclosure must hold; sensitivity itself supplies no grant |
| Recheck an earlier commitment before withholding a useful warning | Coherency, wisdom and no silent loss: balance the actual costs to all affected people within current permission |
| Preserve a well-supported disagreement after the operator praises an inaccurate answer | Alignment, purpose wisdom and Rule 19: approval is not the truth criterion |
| Reveal confidential information whenever many users later approve | Reject as a pillar/authority contradiction; preserve the report and any waiver evidence for constitutional review, never install the lesson |

**Value — candidate gap P23-GAP-01: competing permitted harms.** When both
available actions are permitted, both cause supported consequential harm to
different people, and no purpose statement or scoped policy resolves priority,
this research cannot derive a universal ordering from the four pillars. File the
actual conflict and alternative priority policies as a candidate purpose
amendment. Do not silently invent a total utility score or declare “always
withhold.” The immediate consumer still follows its existing declared floor and
fail direction, providing permitted help and a reachable status. It must not
install a reusable new priority rule while the gap remains unresolved.

**Value — candidate gap P23-GAP-02: residual inferential disclosure.** The
permission requirement is settled. A source policy that authorizes private use
but does not say how much audience inference it permits leaves no measurable
standard for an action whose implications cannot be bounded. First ask the scope
owner to resolve its existing policy. If that requires a new acceptable-risk
policy that the purpose cannot decide, file the concrete question as a candidate
amendment; no experimental accuracy threshold grants that permission. The
unenforceable private-use mode remains inactive under Part 21 §5.

## 8. Comparative recommendation

**Value — recommend B as the design direction, A as the evaluation control,
and C as a bounded independent challenge arm.** B directly represents the missing
question in both code audits: how each permissible choice helps or hurts each
affected person, including through indirect disclosure and withholding. A makes
its extra cost falsifiable; C tests whether additional scrutiny actually helps.
Neither B's structure nor C's debate earns a wisdom claim before measurement.

R5 supplies candidate record fields, local learning gates, grader audits and
preregistered evaluation thresholds. D1 should request the named owner seams
and carry both gap entries into its decision list. This recommendation activates
no semantic blocking reviewer, fleet sharing, experiment spending or private
replay. It does not claim independent Rule 65 convergence.
