### 8.1 Population, cases and preregistration artifact
**Rule — real cases support the claim; fixtures test the controls.** Rules
27/58/65 and Nine §§6–7; **Checks: P23-NF-15/16/17a**. Enroll 120 permitted real
incident clusters for development and 600 disjoint, sealed real incident clusters
for the primary held-out comparison. A cluster groups the same incident,
conversation, related people/history and derived variants; all related cases
stay in one split. Use one randomly selected canonical occasion per cluster in
the primary analysis. Do not inflate independence with repeats, alternate
graders, model seeds or multiple machines. Remaining cross-cluster dependence
must be reported and handled by the frozen clustering method, not ignored.

Assign 100 primary cases to each stratum below using the earliest matching
stratum in this listed order; retain secondary tags and report intersections.
This yields 600 total without double-counting. If available authorized evidence
cannot fill the population, publish the shortage and exploratory observations;

### 8.2 Execution and separation of claims
**Rule — replay cannot dispatch an effect.** **Checks: P23-NF-15/16/17a**.
Run A/B/C once on the same permitted decision-time inputs per primary occasion.
The replay environment has no production dispatch capability, regardless of
model instruction. Future messages, actual outcomes and labels reach only the
grader channel. A new model/provider replay is a fresh permission and budget
check. A sanitized derivative must retain the decision-relevant ambiguity and
permission conditions or be marked ineligible with its semantic loss. Synthetic
positive/negative fixtures exercise decoders, races and control failures in section 11;
they cannot certify real wisdom or count toward the 600 cases.

Offline scores measure decision appropriateness against the frozen permitted
case evidence. The historical observed outcome belongs to the action actually
taken; it is not the observed outcome of A, B or C's unexecuted alternatives.
Run a subsequent prospective local observation phase for the selected candidate
over at least 300 new independent eligible occasions and at least 30 days,
waiting for all required later windows. Initially observe existing authorized
behavior and advisory suggestions. A limited live local trial requires all
owner seams, current authority, independent checks and its applicable activation
approvals; no experimental withholding or disclosure is imposed on real people
solely to obtain a counterfactual. No fleet release follows automatically.

Prospective outcomes without randomized comparable actions support a scoped
observational claim, not causal superiority. A future ethically and
constitutionally admissible randomized protocol would need its own
preregistration; it is not silently implied here. Human comparisons also need a
separate frozen population, tools and time allowance. This protocol cannot by
itself support a super-human wisdom claim.

**Rule — live timing is a separate authorized workload.**
**Checks: P23-NF-06/15/16/19.** `P23-TIMING-01` measures ordinary permitted
replies on the actual intake, queue, judgment and effect route to an operator-owned
test account. It is not the wisdom replay or a counterfactual intervention in a
person’s conversation. Before admission, record a scoped grant naming the account,
verified audience, provider, route, harmless test content, finite resources and
300 A/B pairs (600 distinct reply requests); without it the endpoint is unmeasured.
No third-party recipient, private incident or consequential task is replayed.
The authorized account’s observing client records receipt of the complete reply.

Freeze 300 test inputs spanning the intended ordinary-reply sizes and queue-load
bands, the fixed model/settings and permitted context snapshots. The pairing key
is `(timing protocol digest, case ordinal, route/configuration digest, load band)`.
Each pair contains one fresh A request and one fresh B request using the same
input/context; their arm-specific request and effect identities remain distinct.
Randomize A-first/B-first from a sealed seed within each load band; keep both in
the same scheduled band and record actual load. An unmatched band or changed
configuration invalidates that pair and leaves the endpoint uncertified; retain
it in the report rather than substituting a favorable run. This supports only
the named route, content sizes and tested load conditions.

For each request, a single instrumented client’s monotonic clock records start
immediately before submitting the request to live intake and end when that client
has received the complete delivered reply. This includes ingress, queueing,
judgment, dispatch and delivery; server/model timestamps are diagnostic, not a
substitute. Freeze a 60-second observation window per request. A refusal,
cancellation, timeout, missing reply, incomplete stream or missing clock sample
has unfinished duration `+infinity`; record its elapsed wait and reason. Never
replace it with the timeout duration or drop it. Stop/revocation still wins.

A successful arm’s request admits exactly one reply effect on its tested path;
revoked or refused requests produce no reply and retain their unfinished status. Eight binds
it to the permitted bytes/audience/standing, retains idempotent identity across
retries and forbids a second effect after uncertain dispatch. A/B are two explicitly
authorized test requests, never two executions of one user action. Additional or
unauthorized sends fail integrity. The offline wisdom environment retains no live
credentials or dispatch port; these timing cases supply no wisdom denominator.

With all 300 pairs finished, calculate `d_i = latency_B_i - latency_A_i` in seconds,
sort the 300 differences increasingly. A nearest-rank percentile selects the
observation at one-based position `ceil(q*n)` for fraction `q`; use
`Q95(d) = d_(ceil(0.95 * 300)) = d_(285)` (one-based indexing). This is the
percentile of paired differences, not the difference of two percentiles. Pass
only if `Q95(d) <= 1 second` and there are zero new mandatory semantic-blocking
review invocations. If either arm of any pair is unfinished, do not subtract
infinities: mark that pair unfinished and the whole latency endpoint uncertified.
Report each arm’s finite latencies, every unfinished wait and completion fraction.
No offline timing, selected-candidate-only observation or passing completed subset
certifies this endpoint. Applicable owner deadlines remain binding; the observation
window grants no extra authority or wait time to a real user’s conversation.

Basis: Purpose’s evidence/authority constraints, [least revelation and nothing
outward by default](../00-the-purpose.md#the-purpose); rules 57/65/86/95;
sections 3/10/12’s J/E/O/M grants and real-owner acceptance conditions. The
workload size, window and quantile are agent-owned engineering defaults.

### 8.3 Endpoints and numeric thresholds
**Rule — candidate acceptance bars.** **Checks: P23-NF-13/14/15/16/17a.** These settings may be replaced before
sealing with a recorded reason. After seeing results, any changed threshold or
candidate is a new comparison using fresh held-out cases. No repeated peeking
until significance, no early success stopping and no exclusion of difficult
cases. Early stopping is permitted only for the failure rules below. The primary
comparison is B versus A; C comparisons are exploratory and cannot certify C as
a winner without an independently preregistered confirmatory comparison.

**Paired resampling** estimates uncertainty by repeatedly drawing whole independent
incident clusters with replacement, keeping each selected cluster’s A and B results
together. A **confidence bound** is a one-sided limit computed by a fixed sampling
procedure at its stated coverage level; it is not a probability that the claim is
true. For the primary gain, let `a_i,b_i` be binary case-success indicators and
`gain = mean(b_i - a_i)` across the 600 planned independent units. Draw 10,000
paired samples of 600 units with replacement using the frozen seed; calculate the
gain for each and sort them. The one-sided 97.5% lower bound is the 250th sorted
gain (nearest-rank 2.5% quantile, one-based). Missing execution has indicator zero.
Prespecified higher-level dependence groups must stay together; fewer than 600
independent primary units cannot certify the primary comparison.

The **exact binomial upper bound** is a conservative ceiling for an event rate
computed from the binomial model of independent yes/no opportunities, without a
normal approximation. For `k` events among `n > 0` independent opportunities and
one-sided confidence `1-alpha`, use the Clopper–Pearson bound `U = 1` if `k=n`;
otherwise solve `sum(j=0..k) choose(n,j) U^j (1-U)^(n-j) = alpha` for `U` in
`[0,1]`. Here `alpha=0.05`. With zero events, `U=1-alpha^(1/n)`.
Zero eligible opportunities is undefined. Count unknown/missing classifications
as events for these acceptance bounds; disclose their count separately. If
independence cannot be established, report descriptive rates and leave the bound
uncertified. Multiple variants of one incident do not supply independent trials.

|---|---|

**Value — precision and power limits.** With zero failures in 300 independent
opportunities, the one-sided 95% exact binomial upper bound is
`1 - 0.05^(1/300)`, about 0.994%; in 600 it is about 0.498%. For an individual
100-case stratum it is about 2.951%, so the all-case sample cannot certify a
below-1% rate separately for every stratum. Report those limits explicitly.
If dependence reduces the effective independent population, do not use those
binomial claims; obtain the required independent population or leave the bound
uncertified. Zero observed events is not proof of zero possible harm.

The 600-case design does not promise power for an arbitrary true gain: the
**paired discordance rate** is unknown: it is the fraction of matched cases
where exactly one of A and B succeeds, `(A-only successes + B-only successes)/n`.
It determines how much a paired success difference can vary across samples.
Before sealing, use only the 120 development
clusters to publish a precision/power estimate for the five-point target. The
fixed held-out sample still stops at its declared size; low precision yields no
winner. Confidence intervals support the stated sampled population, not all
people, languages or situations. Required grader-stratum evidence that is absent
remains absent even if the pooled mean passes.

### 8.4 Decision rule and feedback to the design
**Rule — select only what the evidence supports.** **Checks: P23-NF-15/16.** If B satisfies every
applicable replay and grader bar and the B-over-A gain test, proceed to its scoped
prospective assessment. Recommend only locally measured classes after their
prospective bars, the separate live timing endpoint and required later observation
windows also pass. Missing timing authorization/evidence means that endpoint is
unmeasured, not waived by a successful offline comparison.
If B is not better, prefer the less costly adequately performing A or report
no adequate candidate. If neither passes, repair and preregister a new trial.
C's added detections are a reason to plan a confirmatory test, not authority to
install a compulsory reviewer. No pooled quality score hides a harmed minority,
missing standing, unknown outcome or failing class.

Use blinded reviewer findings to identify a recall deficiency, missing actual
input, poor use choice, unsupported reason, bad outcome, invalid grading or
policy gap as distinct causes. Route recall defects to Part 21, grading and
correction work through Nine, metric defects to Part 20, and export/admission
issues to Part 22. Apply section 13’s three dispositions to policy gaps; only a
missing general purpose principle becomes an amendment candidate. No unresolved
priority enters the learner’s reward function.
