## 7. Measurement and outcome accounting

**Rule — Part 20 owns the census and measured joins.**
**Checks: P23-NF-05/08/15/16/18.** Part 23 supplies occasion-to-question,
manifest, criterion and outcome-hook joins to Part 20. Accepted canonical
occasions, including refusal, no effect, cancellation and default, form the
production population. Planned benchmark `(run, scenario version, candidate,
ordinal)` executions form the replay population, where the execution ordinal is
the zero-based position of a planned execution for one scenario and candidate,
distinct from a retry. Repeated model calls, machine
copies and multiple graders do not inflate either. Before-dispatch failures
remain counted; zero denominator is undefined, not success or failure.

Basis: Purpose's evidence and no-silent-loss constraints; rules 13/39/41/58/108;
[Part 20 §7](../20-measurement-ledgers/07-feature-benchmark-and-burn-joins.md),
[R5 §7](research/05-proposals-and-evaluation.md).

| Observation | Required account |
|---|---|
| Recall/input coverage | Accepted occasions; actual manifests; missing authorized context and unknown contribution; never blame judgment for evidence not delivered |
| Sensitivity coverage | Section 2's distinct owner-issued fact/version identity set at pinned source/registry frontiers, shared with NF-03/NF-18; total members, assessed/pending/disputed/unavailable links and UNASSESSED members, first-use attachment violations and broken links separately. Never-recalled unassessed members remain in the denominator and coverage debt, not violations; report missing/unusable assessment identities and oldest unresolved age. Excluded kinds contribute no members, replicas add no duplicate identities and unresolved membership forbids a completeness claim |
| Choice adequacy | Conclusion and reason separately supported, process satisfied, acceptable-choice set and per-party harm evidence; no scalar proxy |
| Actual outcomes | Met/unmet/pending/unverifiable/not-applicable by originally declared window, with causal attribution limits |
| Withholding | Legitimate refusal, avoidable withholding, supported missed help and unknown counterfactual; each with eligible denominator |
| Review burden | Proposed holds, actual blocked effects, override/waiver evidence, reviewer unavailability and missing replies separately |
| Grader quality | Scope validity, fidelity, calibration and pressure response by configuration and stratum; raw disagreement and missingness |
| Local learning | Candidate/trial/support/suspension counts, current grades and pillar support, contrary evidence, recurrence and overdue repair |
| Resources | Total attributed tokens, attempts, failures, retries, queue and end-to-end latency; separate grading and challenge costs plus combined totals |
| Fleet boundary | Eligible offers, refusals, pending corrections and receipts; local counts never claim independent fleet support |

**Rule — compatibility precedes a benchmark comparison.**
**Checks: P23-NF-15/16.** Consume Seven's current owner-resolved task class,
prompt/context, model/settings, floor/output schema and evaluation-contract
identity, Nine's current independent grades and Ten's route-support evidence.
An opaque digest or `measured:false` model description cannot establish measured
compatibility. Source/criterion drift invalidates affected comparisons and opens
bounded review. Part 20's joins and evidence-strength segments remain its own;
self-report and model inference cannot become independent observations in a
headline pass rate. A wisdom criterion mapping cannot change existing owners'
measurement units or claim their pending joins have landed.

Basis: Purpose's evidence constraint; rules 13/58/69/108; Part 20 §7 and its
named Seven/Ten compatibility, Nine evaluation and benchmark-clock dependencies.

**Rule — preserve difficult cases and attribution limits.**
**Checks: P23-NF-15/16/18.** Record eligibility/privacy exclusions before selection
and every missing, stopped, disputed or unavailable member after selection.
Pending and ungradable cases remain in all-enrolled success denominators.
Prospective data measures the action actually taken; an unexecuted alternative
has no observed outcome. Nonrandomized prospective evidence supports a scoped
observational claim, not causal superiority. Unknown usage never becomes zero.
Open waits stay visible when reporting latency; no percentile can improve by
dropping an unanswered conversation.

Basis: Purpose's wisdom and evidence constraint; rules 13/24/58/65/108;
Nine's benchmark evaluation contract and section 8.

**Value — user-centered success.** Better judgment means help that respects
people, keeps commitments and responds to supported later evidence. A readable
reason, high satisfaction or fewer complaints can accompany poor judgment.
Show dimensions and affected parties separately, with explicit limitations.

Basis: Purpose's humane aim, wisdom and trust; rules 24/58/108.
