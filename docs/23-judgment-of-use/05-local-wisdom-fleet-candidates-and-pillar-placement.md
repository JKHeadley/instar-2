## 5. Local wisdom, fleet candidates and pillar placement

**Rule — local learning stays within the constitution.**
**Checks: P23-NF-10/11/12/18.** A LocalWisdomCandidate records the behavioral claim,
operator/relationship/context scope, supporting and contrary cases, current
Grades/standing, applicability, recheck triggers, separate conclusion/reason,
constitutional placement, custody, rollback target and improvement owner.
It cannot change source grants, action floors, purpose, labels or approval powers.
Local learning continues when commons sharing is unavailable.

Basis: Purpose's sovereignty, alignment, wisdom and authority constraint;
rules 57/58/85/108; [R5 §5](research/05-proposals-and-evaluation.md).

| Lifecycle state | Admission and continuing condition |
|---|---|
| Observed candidate | Durable decision/feedback evidence through Nine; retain negative inspections and duplicate-linked reports |
| Scoped trial proposal | Current permitted evidence, bounded behavior and rollback; placement fits; contrary cases assessed |
| Local trial | Existing authority only; shadow evaluation or authorized local preference application; no widening and no experimental harm for data collection |
| Supported local learning | Frozen affected-class bars and required later evidence pass; retain domain limits, unknowns and counterexamples |
| Suspended / rejected | Contradiction, refutation, revoked support, missing required evidence or failed evaluation disables affected current use; preserve originals and owned repair |
| Commons candidate | Separately permitted export projection of a local proposal; no automatic fleet promotion |

A harmless scoped preference may apply under existing local authority; that does
not certify a measured judgment policy. Learning support claims require the
applicable evaluation, not a recurrence threshold or rising confidence alone.

**Rule — a pillar name must carry an argument.**
**Checks: P23-NF-11/14.** The PlacementAssessment contains exact purpose/pillar/
constraint version, scoped learning claim, entailment argument, conflict search,
strongest counterexample, evidence that would falsify it, deciding reviewer and
one of `fits`, `contradicts`, `undecidable-gap`. A word match such as “trust”
is insufficient. Contradictions reject the learning regardless of votes. Before
classifying a gap, apply the purpose’s three-tier check: cite deciding purpose
text, a deployment policy and its register default, or a measured agent-owned
engineering default. Only a question fitting none files a candidate amendment
with incompatible options and operator ownership. No unresolved priority becomes
an admitted lesson; section 13’s competing-harm policy remains pending.

Basis: Purpose's gap rule, alignment and authority constraint; rules 49/108.

| Fictional lesson | Placement reasoning |
|---|---|
| Keep answers short for this operator unless evidence requires detail | Coherency retains a relevant local preference; wisdom bounds it by useful, honest communication |
| Recheck an earlier promise before withholding help | Coherency and no silent loss preserve the commitment; wisdom examines the cost of omission within permission |
| Preserve supported disagreement despite praise for a false answer | Alignment and truth-bearing care reject approval as evidence of truth |
| Reveal private facts when enough people approve | Contradicts trust and authority; rejected, even with many favorable reports |
| Always choose person A's welfare over person B's when both actions are permitted | No universal ordering follows from the pillars; GAP-01 pending, no installed priority |

**Rule — offer a candidate to Part 22, never issue fleet admission.**
**Checks: P23-NF-12/18.** The local handoff contains scoped lesson identity,
conclusion/reason grades, verified standing, independence evidence/unknowns,
negative cases, limits, placement or gap and correction lineage. It stays local
until source/effect owners permit the export. Part 22 owns the wire schema,
destination enrollment, transformations, receipts, cohort sufficiency, cross-agent
independence, weighting, privacy controls, fleet admission and human approval.
Many independent cases, pillar placement and human approval are necessary;
none cures a constitutional contradiction. Part 23 chooses no fleet quorum.

Basis: Purpose's authority constraint and alignment; rules 28/57/86/108;
[Part 22 inspected handoff](https://github.com/JKHeadley/instar-2/blob/9d44f4f3186dd99056a843cf29724cb38ebceb39/docs/22-the-commons/research/02-what-flows-and-what-must-not.md),
[R4 §6](research/04-compare-and-contrast.md).

Default content-free export excludes raw lesson prose, reasons, cases, source
hashes and global identifiers. Only permitted categories and destination-scoped
opaque handles pass the commons contract. A richer scenario needs separate
permission and an account of meaning lost in transformation. Unknown independence
stays unknown; cloning one incident across agents does not multiply support.

Fleet return rechecks current local scope, pillar version, applicability and
support; admission is evidence, not local authority. Local refutation suspends
affected local support immediately and offers an eligible correction through
Part 22. Queue/receipt/retry state remains durable and no commons outage silently
closes correction propagation. Raw private provenance is never exported just to
make a fleet independence claim look stronger.
