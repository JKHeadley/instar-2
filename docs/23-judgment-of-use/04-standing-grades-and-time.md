## 4. Standing, grades and time

**Rule — verify standing for each graded claim.**
**Checks: P23-NF-07/08/09.** Nine accepts a wisdom Grade only with the verified
principal, authority evidence, role, criterion, affected subject/relationship
scope, permitted evidence access, validity/revocation frontier, conflicts and
grader identity. Automated session senders are principals too. A report outside
standing remains durable evidence that may trigger investigation; it is not an
admitted authoritative grade. Historical standing is resolved at the actual time;
revoked powers cannot justify current reliance.

Basis: Purpose's authority constraint; rules 28/29/58/85/108;
[Nine §§6–7](../13-the-verification-holders.md).

| Assessor | Evidence it may supply | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Own preference, experienced outcome and decisions within granted scope | Cannot make a premise true or erase another person's harm |
| Affected subject | Their experience and consent within recognized scope | Cannot grant another subject's consent or dictate a fleet norm |
| Expert | Domain-scoped factual/methodological assessment | Expertise grants neither source access nor constitutional authority |
| Delegated model | Bounded interpretation of permitted evidence under registered criterion | A semantic grade is inference, not self-proved observation or independence |
| Unverified reporter | A reproducible allegation or correction to investigate | A claimed name creates no operator or grading standing |

**Rule — keep conclusion, reason and outcome independently testable.**
**Checks: P23-NF-08/09.** Use Nine's existing dimension values, adding per-party
harm evidence and scoped standing through its requested specialization. Keep
original criterion, source frontier, availability, grading Decision and missing
references. Do not build a competing scalar Grade or a winner-by-popularity fold.

Basis: Purpose's wisdom Value; rules 58/108; Nine's Grade and affected-claim review.

| Dimension | Values and required interpretation |
|---|---|
| Conclusion | supported / contradicted / unverifiable / not-applicable |
| Reason | supported / contradicted / unverifiable / not-applicable, independently of conclusion |
| Outcome | met / unmet / pending / unverifiable / not-applicable against the declared subject and window |
| Process | satisfied / violated / unverifiable for each mandatory requirement |
| Completeness | complete / incomplete / disputed; complete does not mean correct |

A right answer based on a false premise remains reason-contradicted. A legitimate
refusal may satisfy process and leave the goal unmet. A pleased recipient does
not cancel a confidentiality breach. Concurrent incompatible grades and different
subjects' outcomes remain attributable disputes. Untaken alternatives have no
observed outcome merely because the taken action worked.

**Rule — later outcome evidence can defeat immediate approval.**
**Checks: P23-NF-09/18.** Append later observations and linked reassessments;
never rewrite the original Decision/Grade. Supported later harm outranks initial
satisfaction as evidence about that outcome. A later unsupported compliment does
not outrank an observed injury by timestamp. Refuting a reason opens re-derivation
of the conclusion even when the conclusion seems plausible. Missing evidence
makes a claim unverifiable, not false. Recurrence requires investigation of the
underlying failure, rather than resetting confidence after every temporary repair.

Basis: Purpose's wisdom and coherency; rules 24/58/85/108; Nine affected-claim and
feedback lifecycle, including requested correction-class composition in section 12.

**Value — observation settings.** Agent-owned initial horizons are immediate,
7 days and 30 days. A known later promise gets a later declared hook and cannot
be called mature at 30 days. Boot recovery and a five-minute bounded inspection
cadence reconcile missing hooks and overdue work. These timings can be changed
by measurement before the experiment is sealed; they are not universal policy.

Basis: Purpose's no-silent-loss constraint and “Who decides what”; rules 55/58/95;
[R5 §§4/8](research/05-proposals-and-evaluation.md), section 8 protocol.

**Rule — withdrawal invalidates support and preserves history.**
**Checks: P23-NF-09/10/18.** Source correction, grader disqualification,
contrary outcomes and revoked standing reopen exactly the dependent claims.
Suspend affected current lesson/routing support before new consequential reliance,
then re-derive through Nine. Do not suspend unrelated knowledge or erase original
records. Closure requires every due assessment's disposition; a timer is not
success. Feedback dispositions and an actual improvement run remain distinct
from sending a reassuring response.

Basis: Purpose's no-silent-loss and evidence constraints; rules 24/85/95/108.
