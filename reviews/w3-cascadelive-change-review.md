# Change review — w3-cascadelive: a summary result proven over the output cap is a failed attempt, not UNKNOWN, and the next span is shorter, so the confidence cascade is reached live

Outcome: The proof room on cint-L9 never accepted a summary: summaryThrough null, 4 summaryPending, 7 summary-writer calls, 3 Jev faithfulness checks and zero summary reviews. Every writer call after 03:03 (#483 2312, #487 3662, #491 2625, #493 4832 output tokens) finished with a success frame but ran past the 2048-token output cap. The provider called each one uncertain, and the journal kept it as an UNKNOWN charge. An UNKNOWN charge makes the summary pass wait out its recovery delay, then floors every later frontier. With no summary accepted, every later span started at the first turn and only grew, so no candidate ever reached Jev or the stronger review. sumaccept's cascade was correct, but it was never reached; its tests used a stub writer that never ran over the cap. Fix, in the preview journal only: the journal already records each call's physical outcome row before the adapter classifies it. When that row proves a finished, successful result over the cap (localLimit output-cap, exit 0, result/success, not an error), the projection marks the open reservation as proven. The summary pass settles it as summary-failed with the reason "summary output over the cap" and the provider's reported usage, before the UNKNOWN gate and at each attempt. It then offers only frontiers shorter than any span that ran over the cap from the same base (cleared when a summary is accepted). The retry stays inside the pass's eight attempts and maxCalls. A settlement without its outcome row is refused. A call with no outcome row, or without a finished result, stays UNKNOWN exactly as before. status.lastSummaryFailure shows the fixed reason of a failure newer than the last accepted summary. Evidence: tests/preview/summary-overcap-cascade.test.ts replays the proof room's recorded shapes verbatim: the four over-cap outcome rows and usage, both #482 writer outputs, the #483 {"reply"} envelope (refused as malformed), the Jev 0.22/0.16/0.30 replies and the no-result-frame outcome.
