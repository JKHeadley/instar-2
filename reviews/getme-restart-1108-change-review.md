# Change review — Launch getMe waits out a slow Telegram instead of refusing

Subject base: 0576b5a93c2f8838542fee928f0933d3143454cf
Review state: open
Reviewed content: none
Outcome: a routine runner restart no longer takes the agent offline for minutes when Telegram answers one getMe slowly. On 2026-11-08 01:19-01:25 three relaunches in a row were refused "preview: bot identity refused" while getMe took 21 s once and answered 504 Gateway Timeout once. The cause was two-part: tests/preview/journal-agent.mjs asked getMe once (timeoutMs 30000) and refused on anything but a verified identity, and src/assembly/telegram-bot-api-bridge.mjs reported every non-200 getMe answer as the bare stage invalid-response, so a 504, a 401 and a wrong bot were indistinguishable. The bridge now adds the HTTP status (an integer, never the body) to a non-200 getMe answer and keeps the fixed stage enum. The new tests/preview/bot-identity.mjs asks up to three times within 90 s in total (each try at most 30 s, pauses 2 s then 5 s, the operator stop and signals checked after every pause) when there is no answer (fetch-timeout, fetch-failure, body-read, child-exit, spawn-refused, launch-refused) or a temporary server failure (5xx or 429, by the existing temporaryPollStatus). Another bot id, a malformed 200 answer, a sealed-capture failure, and 401, 404 or any other 4xx still refuse at once with no pause. The launch error now names each attempt's stage and status.
