## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required outcome is that a deferred loop is worked on its revisit with no inbound message, its result is held for the operator's next message, and status says so. Every piece of that already existed: the tick, the per-root interval, the held-report delivery and the status line. Two things stopped it. A validator refused the durable row of the very call that produced the result, and a sentence missing from the work question let the model avoid reporting. The simplest robust route is to fix those two points and add nothing. This proposal is that route: one predicate widened for two ids bound to a live reservation, and one sentence. No guard, no retry, no new record kind, no change to the interval or the grant.
