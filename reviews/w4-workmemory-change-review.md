## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required outcome is that a scheduled work step can see the facts its own summary retained. The simplest robust route through existing mechanisms is exactly the reviewer's smallest fix: carry the ordinary answer projection's already-reviewed filtering, source labelling and redaction of `summary.memoryItems` into the work packet, under the bound the write side already enforces — no new store, retrieval service, model call, constant or approval, and the projection shared rather than copied so the two consumers cannot drift again. This proposal is that route, with one addition: the fit ladder gains a middle rung. The named credible failure it prevents is that a work packet which fits today with its prose and messages would, once the facts are added, exceed the same bound and fall to the existing "no memory at all" rung — so the grounding fix would itself remove grounding in the pressure case. The simpler two-rung route cannot handle that, because its only alternative to the full packet is no memory; the cost is one `for` loop over three named levels in the same function, with no new state, flag or recovery path. Guards: start — the step still opens only through its existing durable reservation inside `gate()`; end-state — the packet is still refused when even the no-memory rung exceeds `maxBytes` (`return false`, unchanged); limit — the write-side bounds (20 items, 300 bytes each) and `maxBytes` are both unchanged, and the ladder yields to them. No autonomous-completion claim is made here.
