## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required outcome is that an open loop the agent owns always has a next step, and the simplest robust route through the existing mechanisms is to let the cadence the journal already computes for `waitsOn: "nothing"` serve every wait declared at creation, leaving the existing `resume` arm to hold a dependency once a step has actually recorded one. That is this proposal: two lines in `obligationSchedule`, no new store, no new surface, no new per-value branch and no narrowing of any ability. The applicable guards are the existing ones — the step's bounded token reservation at the append (start), the recorded result that moves the loop on (end state), and the revisit cadence plus `resume` (limit).
80/20: 0 must-fix, 1 note — the `openLoops` decision prompt still offers `"nothing" | "operator" | "external" | "date"` with no gloss of what each means, so a model can keep declaring `operator` for work it could finish itself; that costs one extra bounded step per such loop now instead of losing the loop, and the wording is worth a separate model-facing change with its own live evidence.
