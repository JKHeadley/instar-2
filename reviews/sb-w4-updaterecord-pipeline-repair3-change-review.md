## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required outcome is that a revision of an objected reply never costs the operator the finished results that reply carries, while every check still reviews the exact text sent. The simplest robust route is to keep the runner's result lines out of the reviser's hands and attach them again verbatim before the revision review: one helper (also used where the lines are first attached), one filter of the lines the reply carries, one strip of the draft and one append to the candidate. No new row, field, flag, model call or gate. Rejected: (a) teaching the reviser in prompt prose to keep the lines, which is willpower, not structure, and the live reviser already had them in front of it; (b) loosening `withheldReports` to treat a paraphrase as delivery, which would mark results delivered on the model's word and break the verbatim evidence rule the replay relies on; (c) relaxing D1c, which would hide a real fault; (d) skipping the revision when results are attached, which narrows the agent's ability to correct its own prose (constitution revision 12).
