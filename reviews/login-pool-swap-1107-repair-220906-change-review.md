A signed-out observation and a quota or provider hold now keep separate recovery evidence inside the same pool record: the sign-in fingerprint and its 12-hour deadline live in the record's `signedOut` map, and `holds` keeps only `provider-limit` and `usage-threshold`. A fresh sign-in (changed fingerprint) or the ceiling clears only the sign-in evidence, so a still-binding weekly or unknown-reset provider hold keeps the login out of selection; a later provider limit no longer rewrites the sign-in entry, so the pool never writes a record its own validator refuses. Both causes survive in either observation order and across a reopen. 1.x (JKHeadley/instar 1a5def9c9, src/core/QuotaPoller.ts) keeps `cliLoginExpiredHolds` apart from its quota observations; this follows that split. Because the two causes no longer share one reason, a login that cannot serve at all (signed out or excluded) now moves with the effective reason `member-unavailable`, so the usage-threshold destination-headroom brake (1.x SwapAntiThrash, for a login that could still serve) does not strand a signed-out login whose quota is also over the cutoff.
