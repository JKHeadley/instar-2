## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: Required outcome: an accepted question interrupted by a host restart is answered once. The simplest robust route reuses the existing restart recovery, queue, admission, per-call reservation pattern and in-host lost-worker conditions. It adds one durable counter, because the only proof that no tool ran lived in memory and was lost on restart. Without it, the choice is never re-running (the defect) or re-running blind (the duplicate-effect risk 1.x accepted). Start guard: the original durable intake and a dispatch-time count saved atomically before the adapter sees input. End guard: one correlated completed answer, settled once and sent through the existing unique journal send. Limit guards: three dispatches, remaining allowance, parent lifetime, stop, confirmed retirement and the ownership fence. No unattended shipped-path completion is claimed.
