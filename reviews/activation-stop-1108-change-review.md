# Change review — Activation end date is a reminder, never a stop

Subject base: 91e7e5321a9104bad2ac9be9da52e01fca2d5a1a
Review state: open
Reviewed content: none
Outcome: the live 2.0 agent no longer stops answering on a calendar date. Before, the activation record's end date was a hard stop in five layers: the journal worker's gate latched a 'trial expired' stop and threw; the journal fold refused turn, reply, reminder, owed-result and notice rows at or after the end; the runner's loops, send admission, media, proofs, updater and due-wake treated the end as stopped and the exit path appended the same stop; both subscription doorway validators (Claude and Codex) refused the activation record once now >= expiresAt, so a restart was refused before launch; and conversation-session work halted on a parent lifetime copied from the end date, which persists and could not be extended by a restart. Now the activation record stays the authority record (who approved what, matched exactly against the journal and the reviewed end constants) and its end date only drives the existing fixed notice, reworded as a one-time reminder that also fires after the date when the runner was down. Renewal and cap raises are accepted after the date. Every other refusal stays: the operator's stop, revoked, unsealed or wrong-operator authority, a grant's own recorded expiry, wrong account or artifact or policy, an unreviewed end value, and a clock before the record was observed.
Affected rules: 4, 14, 15, 26, 37, 42, 55, 60, 74, 77, 86, 95, 102, 103, 113, 114, 116, 119
Affected floors: secrets — unchanged; no credential, token or custody path is touched; spend cap — unchanged; the journal's call, reply, turn and byte caps still gate every call and the session-work call allowance still renews only per epoch with each epoch's own finite budget; stop — kept and strengthened: the operator stop latch, the stop file and signals still end the runner, and stop and approval challenges are no longer clamped to the activation end, which would have made a stop challenge issued after the end already expired; no duplicate sends — unchanged; the reminder keeps its intent-before-dispatch row and its once-per-end check in both the worker and the fold, proven across restart and an UNKNOWN send; durable intake — kept: past the end the runner keeps reading and admitting updates instead of leaving them at Telegram

Multi-machine posture: machine-local behaviour on the lease holder; the journal rows involved are the existing replicated ones and no new store or shared field is added. The session lifetime is computed per launch on the machine that launches.
