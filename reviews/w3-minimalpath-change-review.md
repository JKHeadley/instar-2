## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required outcome is that a single-machine preview answers the operator once past a cap, without claiming anything it has not observed and without loosening the peer requirement anywhere else. The simplest robust route through existing mechanisms is the one taken: Part Eleven's existing verdict gets the one input the design already names (the installed shape), the runner reads three states it already holds (the generated register pin, its conversation claim, its verified fence), and the operator's acceptance rides the existing sealed authority record and its existing message-authenticity check. No new store, key, service, journal row, send path or approval surface. The added machinery is one resolver and one shape check; the failure they prevent is the one the design calls out by name, a caller dropping the peer by saying it is single-machine.
