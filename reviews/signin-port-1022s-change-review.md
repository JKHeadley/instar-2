Side effects: The normal Chrome window remains open for the account owner. Undo and recovery: Revert the code without deleting custody, repair records or journals. Do not restore an obsolete credential to hide a failure. An interrupted browser attempt rechecks the staged credential before publication. A crash retains account/browser locks; their owner must establish the original process ended before release. No age-based takeover or blind retry is introduced. A wrong identity remains held, never published.
Multi-machine posture: Deliberately machine-local Keychain, dedicated Chrome profile, custody and repair locks. An account's custody lock serializes roots which share that profile, and a browser lock excludes simultaneous repairs in the same browser directory. No credentials, browser data or repair authority move to peers.
