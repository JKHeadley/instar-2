# Change review — sb-w4-rollback repair 1: a bounded wait is not ended by one unknown reading

Outcome: The sb-w4-rollback full-suite gate (HEAD 8d418b25, 06:07 PDT) failed two cases, and both are the same shape: a single load-sensitive reading decided a verdict that already had a bounded wait available to it. (1) `scripts/resource-owner.mjs` `cleanupTree`: an incomplete or failed census is unknown and must never read as quiescence, but it is not a verdict either — under the parallel suite a candidate's working directory or its start evidence can be briefly unreadable, and the wait ended on the first such reading, so a launch whose members were in fact reclaimed was recorded `cleanup: 'unresolved'`, keeping its durable ledger row and its Six debit reserved. The unknown reading now retries inside the same 20-attempt/100 ms wait; a wait that never produces a complete census still ends unresolved, and nothing unknown is signalled. (2) `tests/preview/tool-turn.mjs` `unmountScratch`: right after a volume is written macOS can hold it busy (its own indexer, the disk arbitration daemon) and `hdiutil detach -force` refuses; one refusal is not a stuck volume, yet a single attempt failed the turn that wrote the most (`attachScratch` throws on it, and `pruneToolTurns` counts the turn failed and keeps its directory under the mount). The unmount is retried inside a bounded wait (6 attempts, 400 ms apart), and a volume still mounted after that wait is reported exactly as before. This is the class `docs/defects/full-suite-load-timeouts.md` already repairs at source rather than quarantines (there: a p95 of ten samples is their maximum, so one stall decided a flat-cost bound; here: one unknown reading decided a bounded wait). Gate evidence at 8d418b25: `tests/integration/resource-owner.test.ts` › `reclaims a recorded detached descendant that left the process group…` failed at its `result.resources` match, with its `alive(detached)` assertion one line earlier passing — so the member was reclaimed, and `membership` is a common line in the printed diff, leaving `cleanup`/`allocation`/`leakedDescendants` as the differing field; `tests/preview/tool-turn.test.ts` › `bounds a workspace's whole storage…` failed at `expect(unmountScratch(turn)).toBe(true)` immediately after filling the volume. The gate's own `.test-results.json` (2,325,414 bytes) exceeds the Studio file API's 1 MiB ceiling, so the exact printed diff could not be read from this machine; the diagnosis is taken from the two code paths and is named as such rather than asserted as measured. The two new cases are the reproducers, and the census one fails on the base source (1 census instead of >=3).
