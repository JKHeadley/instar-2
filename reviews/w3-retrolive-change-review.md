## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required behavior is that a retrospective pass COMPLETES on a live root, with its duties inspected and its accounting honest, while the output cap, the fail-closed direction and the owed set stay exactly as they were. The simplest robust route is four corrections to existing mechanisms and no new one: measure the bytes-per-token constant for the call it governs instead of the call it was borrowed from; price the rows a case already owes in the estimate that already exists; bound the per-pass deferral list under the bound of the record it is already decoded into; and let the existing per-case omission be the disposition for a row or a case the answer got wrong, instead of refusing the pass. No new retry layer, ledger, classifier, row kind or flag is added, and the two pieces of machinery that ARE new each prevent a named failure the simpler route cannot: the start-and-widen direction prevents a first ask on an unmeasured root from paying a failed call per step down (the fresh root's 09:28 failure, which no narrowing route can help because nothing precedes it), and the per-pass budget in the packet prevents the ask from stating a size the plan did not use (live passes 10, 11 and 12, narrowed in the plan and told the whole bound).
80/20: 1 must-fix taken, 3 notes accepted as residue. MUST-FIX taken: the real model's second pass overshot the cap at 2133 tokens on a 3459-byte ask — the widening measurement, left unclamped, would repeat that, so the widened budget is clamped by the whole bound and the bound itself is re-measured. Residue accepted: (a) throughput — a pass now asks about 3 to 6 cases, so Justin's 980-case backlog needs many passes; the grade rows are now the whole per-case cost and a compact shape for them is the obvious next unit; (b) the authorization, comparison and closure rows are not row-isolated, because no recorded or observed failure names one; (c) a pass that accounts for nothing (neither inspected nor omitted anywhere) is recorded complete with zero inspections, which is pre-existing and visible, and the honest place to tighten it is the live I1a check.
