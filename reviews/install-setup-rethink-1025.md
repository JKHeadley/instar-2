Exact-owner checks held every completed negative. This is evidence for retaining those owners,
not evidence of model adequacy. Existing model input already includes booleans, but the renderer
deliberately omits the aggregate exact-owner result, supplies heterogeneous observations and long
digests, and asks the model to reconcile them. The selected change is a complete named-check
contract produced from owner operations, not merely more prompt instructions or exposing the
test's `exact`/`expected` fields. Projection fidelity is a separate acceptance obligation.

No measured improvement is claimed. The two-minute total supervision target is an engineering
default for the next qualification, not a result or an operator policy. Three repetitions must
meet both the correctness bar and that timing target on every supported ordinary host. The
20-minute attempt limit remains only a safety brake. Failure stays visible for the owning build
unit; the installer does not transfer diagnosis or model selection to the person.

## Rule interpretation

Rule 38's “watching each step and validating it” does not require the model to own cryptographic
commit authority. It does require more than recording that a call happened. Treating a model's
output as a distinct advisory signal can preserve its provenance; it cannot establish successful
validation when that output is empty, wrong or unavailable. Rule 38 alone does not explicitly require a veto: a qualified advisory validator with separately
recorded disagreements is a possible reading, with Rule 4 owners enforcing exact state. It does
not cure the measured inadequacy. Rules 42/95 still forbid consuming an actual gate hold as
success merely because its retry budget ended. The selected route retains that closed gate and
the model's ability to narrow allowed actions (57), without claiming every watcher must be one. Deterministic exact checks remain necessary even when the model accepts.

The glossary's identity/security arm applies independently of irreversibility. Therefore delaying
supervision until the subscription session cannot legalize the unsupervised identity preparation
that creates that session's authority. Manual placement has a narrow existing allowance and
cannot cover automated creation, recovery or append. These are constitutional constraints, not
an invitation to add a second onboarding engine or another approval ceremony.

## ELI16

Setup's safety checks already know whether the keys, backup and signed records are right. The
small AI checker has been unreliable when reading their mixed reports. Give it a short, complete
list of named check results with real receipts, and test that simpler job. It must approve good
installs as well as catch bad ones before this design can ship. Until then, no working-installer
claim is made.

## UX Impact

One install command on Mac or Linux chooses the local checker automatically. No paid login,
GPU, Mac relay, model-selection question or model-output inspection is added. Correct setup must
finish its supervision within the measured two-minute target; download and existing protected
enrollment/recovery interactions are reported separately. Holds retain progress, explain the
next repair action and resume from the original receipts without repeating completed effects.
