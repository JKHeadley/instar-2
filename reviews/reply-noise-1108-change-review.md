The live 217 real-task reply of 2026-11-08 11:54 PST ended with "Approved model login 2 was unavailable and was removed from selection..." and three "Follow-up on ...: answered above." lines about older promises it never answered. Recorded journals are not re-validated against the new rule, so old rows replay unchanged.
