# Change review — w3-fixedtrim: the reply-review reserve is sized from what the review really needs

Subject base: 4bf0c73b7910184bbce8065bcff4f9652c8f83d0

Reviewed content: none
Outcome: w3-defaultcap measured that a fresh root at the approved default context limit (32768) leaves about 1.5 KiB for the operator's message and all of its history: the reply-review reserve is a flat `min(8192, floor(L/4))` = 8192, so one turn's whole prompt gets 24576 bytes, and the parts every answer carries measure 23013 of them. Live on 2026-10-01 such a root answered one message and held the next ("summary unavailable: prompt overflow"); every working room was raised to 409600 by hand. This change sizes the reserve from what the reply review actually costs instead of a quarter of the limit, and shortens three packet blocks that repeat what another part of the same prompt already states. Measured on this build: the reserve falls 8192 -> 6845 (4096 one-reply bound + 2749 review-only parts), the always-sent parts fall 23013 -> 22889, so one turn's room rises 24576 -> 25923 and the room left for the message and its history rises 1563 -> 3034 (1.94x). The derived floor falls 30684 -> 29734. A new test drives a twenty-turn conversation on a fresh root at the default limit with the real briefing and reply review wired: every turn is answered, nothing is held for size, no history is set aside, the rolling summary compacts faithfully, and the obligation guide now survives through turn 4 where it previously yielded from turn 3. Three sub-steps were attempted and reverted on evidence, each named in the decisions below: the subscription system prompt cannot be touched here (it is inside the activation record's invocation-policy digest), the standing instruction message buys no room at all (the derivation cancels it against the reserve), and four wordings that looked like repetition are load-bearing contracts or live blocker evidence. The honest conclusion is that the fixed parts are near-irreducible within this brief's constraints; the room came from the reserve.

## Closing block

simplestRobustRoute: the required behaviour is that a fresh root at the shipped default context limit can hold an ordinary conversation — every turn answered, nothing held for size, compaction and the rolling summary working — without a hand-raised limit. The simplest robust route is to stop reserving a flat quarter of the limit for the reply review and reserve what the review actually costs instead: two measured constants, one derived expression at the one place the reserve was already computed, and the existing floor derivation picking the new value up for free. This proposal is that route. The wording trims the brief also asks for were pursued second and are 124 bytes of the 1471-byte gain, because almost every other part of the prompt turned out to be pinned by something real: the system prompt by the activation digest, the mind-rule rows by the rule book, the purpose excerpts by their SHA-256 pins, the capability note's items by the register generation, and five further sentences by contract tests or by the live model quoting them as evidence — each recorded as a decision above rather than left as a guess. The added machinery is two tests. The named credible failure they prevent is the live one repeating silently: the reserve and the fixed parts are numbers, and a number drifts the moment a rule row, a briefing excerpt or a guidance block grows — which is exactly how 32768 became unservable with no change to the limit at all. Their operating cost is two offline runs per suite; their recovery cost is deleting a file. Both are proven in both directions: restoring the flat 8192 reserve makes the twenty-turn test fail with the guide surviving only turns 1 and 2, and makes the headroom assertion fail.
80/20: 0 must-fix, 4 notes — (1) the brief's aim of "fixed parts at or below half of the turn's prompt room" is arithmetically unreachable within its own constraints, and the report states the floor rather than approximating it: the subscription system prompt (3039, activation digest), the thirteen mind-rule rows (2711, pinned to the rule book), the three purpose excerpts as packet entries (2946, pinned to the constitution by digest), the generated capability-note items (1438, generated from the register per Rules 78 and 84) and the request-envelope scaffold (1507) already sum to 11641, which is 89.8% of half the 25923-byte room, before a single JSON field shape in the decision guidance is counted. (2) the gain is 1.94x, not an order of magnitude, and it is dominated by the reserve; the fixed parts moved 124 bytes. (3) the summary step's own ceiling (SUMMARY_MAX_PROMPT_BYTES, 24576) does not scale with the context limit and is untouched by this change: it measured 22132 on a fresh root's first turn and now measures 22049, so it stays the thinner of the two margins however the context limit moves. The new conversation test asserts every summary prompt fits it. (4) the review prompt's duplicated rule text (2171 bytes, one-for-one on the turn's room) is the next lever and is reported with its measurement.
